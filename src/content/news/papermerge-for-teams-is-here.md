Today I'm launching **Papermerge for Teams**: one shared document archive for
your whole company.

Since I started working on Papermerge Cloud, this has been the goal: a document
archive that a small or mid-size company can set up in minutes, without a
consultant and without a six-month rollout, and that still holds up when the tax
auditor asks questions.

## Enterprise features, without the enterprise baggage

Enterprise document management systems like DocuWare or M-Files are powerful,
but that power comes at a price. You usually need a sales call just to find out
what they cost, a partner to set them up, and training before your team can use
them.

Papermerge for Teams gives you the core features a growing team actually
needs: cabinets, user permissions, inbound email, custom metadata, audit logs
and automatic filing.
The difference is how it feels to use them. The interface is designed to be
intuitive from the first minute, and the price is on the website for anyone to
see.

## What you get

- **Your own workspace.** Every company gets its own address, like
  `acme.app.papermerge.com`, used by your company only.
- **Enterprise-level features.** Cabinets for each department, user
  permissions, inbound email, custom metadata, audit logs and automatic filing.
- **Easy to use.** An intuitive interface and a pleasant experience are at the
  center of the design, not an afterthought. Your team can start working
  without training.
- **Built for GoBD.** Compliance comes first. GoBD is the set of German rules
  for keeping business records digitally over the long term. I use it as the
  baseline because its requirements are general enough to be useful in many
  other countries too.
- **Hosted in the EU.** Your documents are stored in Frankfurt, Germany.

## Simple, transparent pricing

No sales call, no hidden fees, no per-module licenses:

- **€39 a month** covers up to 3 users and 100 GB of storage.
- **€9 a month** for each additional user.

That's it. A team of ten pays €102 a month.
